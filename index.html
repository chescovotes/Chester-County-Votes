How It Works

Three steps. Zero cost. One better election day.

STEP ONE

Cast your ballot

Vote at your polling place on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, like you always do.

STEP TWO

Keep your proof

Hang on to your “I Voted” sticker or your ballot receipt. That's all you need.

STEP THREE

Shop local

Walk into any participating business, show your sticker or receipt, and enjoy the offer.
